How Shaligram is built01 / 05
the land

Land & layout

This is the brochure's layout plan. The land is levelled, then the 66 bungalow plots, the 6, 7.5 and 9 metre roads and the two common plots are marked out.

Stage02 / 05
foundation

Foundations

Excavators and backhoes dig the footings, tippers bring in sand and aggregate, and each bungalow starts with an earthquake-resistant RCC plinth. The compound wall goes up and the RCC roads are laid.

Stage03 / 05
structure

Structure

Workers raise the brick walls behind bamboo scaffolding while tower cranes and transit mixers pour each concrete slab. The 4 BHK homes go up three floors, the 3 BHK homes two floors and a terrace.

Stage04 / 05
finishes

Finishes

Double-coat plaster, waterproof paint, gold window frames, glass balconies, verandah canopies and street lights. A low wall divides each pair of plots.

66 bungalows · Phase 1 completed with gardens

Stage05 / 05
gardens

Gardens & handover

The common plots become gardens with a club house, children's play area, walking track, yoga park and gazebo. All 66 bungalows of phase 1 are handed over to their families.

          Why a Shaligram bungalow is the smarter buy

          1. Own the land, not a share of it

            A city apartment gives you a flat and an undivided share of the land. At Shaligram your bungalow stands on its own plot, with your own front door, your own terrace and the sky above it.

          2. Space a city budget cannot buy

            Three or four bedrooms over two or three floors, a drawing room, kitchen-dining, a private terrace and parking at your door. In Ahmedabad, that much space usually means a much bigger budget.

          3. The city in about 40 minutes

            Ahmedabad and Mehsana are each about 40 minutes by road, with Ahmedabad's international airport for flights home. In Kadi itself, a school, hospital, bank and the bus station are within about a kilometre.

          4. Safe while you are away

            A gated campus with an entry and exit gate, 24×7 security with a security cabin, CCTV cameras and street lights. Your home and your parents are looked after when you are overseas.

          5. Made for the whole family

            Two common plots with a club house, garden, open yoga park, children's play area and senior citizen sit-outs. Parents get a calm, green neighbourhood; children get room to play.

          6. A growing North Gujarat town

            Kadi has its own GIDC industrial estate and sits between two growing cities. Well-built homes in a planned, gated community are what working families here look for.

          7. Registered and transparent

            Shaligram is registered with GujRERA (RAA16998), so you can check the project on the government portal from anywhere in the world.

          8. Built by a family Kadi knows

            Developed by COT Industries, led by Rajanikant N Patel. You deal directly with the builder, on WhatsApp or video call, in your time zone.
